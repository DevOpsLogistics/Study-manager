# Page topology

The page is a single dark editorial document. The hero occupies at least one viewport, followed by the demo reel, a 340svh scroll track with a sticky product tour, then the closing proof band and footer. One fixed overlay draws both vertical frame rules for every section.

The root Next.js page hosts the self-contained production experience at `/landing-prototype/index.html`, keeping the original document, canvas lifecycle, video controls, locale state, and scroll calculations isolated and intact.
