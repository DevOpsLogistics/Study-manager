# Behaviors

- EN/VI swaps text in place without recreating WebGL canvases.
- Hero beams load after first paint and fade in over 900ms.
- Terminal lines update continuously in the hero mock window.
- Demo controls support play/pause, seeking by chapter, fullscreen, and keyboard focus.
- Tour chapters advance from scroll progress and can also be selected directly.
- The app window crossfades and reshapes between board, agent grid, and focus-expand states.
- Reduced-motion preferences disable nonessential transitions and smooth scrolling.
- Desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts share a 47.5rem primary responsive breakpoint.
